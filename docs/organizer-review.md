# MyFTCSpace organizer review

The public site is [MyFTCSpace](https://delaware-ftc-hq.vercel.app/). The separate [fictional workspace preview](https://delaware-ftc-hq.vercel.app/review) is intended for evaluating the interface with sample records. It is not an approved team workspace. Do not enter names, contact details, student information or other real team data.

The preview runs locally in the browser tab. Changes are temporary and are reset when the page is reloaded. Role choices are simulations; they do not create accounts or grant actual permissions. The preview does not demonstrate live multi-user chat, private messages, invitations, shared files or server-side persistence. Those require configured services and separate hosted tests with fictional accounts.

The public [Robot Lab](https://delaware-ftc-hq.vercel.app/robot-lab) is available without a team account. Its calculations, models, routes and generated code are examples or estimates that require qualified review and controlled testing on the actual robot. Enter only fictional or non-sensitive technical information in a review.

The deployed `/api/deployment/status` shows the current build fingerprint, team-access state and coarse configuration flags. Configuration flags do not prove actual provider behavior. Real team data remains behind the release gate until the responsible adults and organizations complete the required review and the actual services are configured and verified. No FIRST, Delaware FTC or school endorsement is claimed.

For the existing checklist mapping and the outstanding decisions, see [release evidence](release-evidence.md), [release review](release-review-2026-11-02.md) and [launch acceptance](launch-acceptance.md).
